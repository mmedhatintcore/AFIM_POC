About AFIM

Al Ahly Financial Investments Management (AFIM) was the first asset-management company established in Egypt, founded in 1994 under Capital Market Law 95/1992, license no. 21.

The firm manages a diversified family of mutual funds — money market, fixed income, balanced, equity, Sharia-compliant and gold — alongside tailored portfolios for major institutions covering a range of asset classes and strategies.

Vision

To be the leading and most trusted asset-management firm in the Egyptian and regional markets.

Mission

To grow and protect our clients' wealth through disciplined, research-driven investment and fully integrated risk management.

Values

Integrity, transparency, loyalty to client objectives, and prudence in volatile markets.

The Board of Directors sets strategy and oversees governance.
